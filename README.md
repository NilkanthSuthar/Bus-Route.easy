# 🚍 Bus-Route.easy

**A real-time city transit tracking app built for the Smart India Hackathon 2023 (Top 6/500 finalist)**  
Helps commuters access live bus locations, accurate trip planning, and depot information in an intuitive, map-based interface.

## 📌 Overview

This project was developed to solve the problem of uncertainty in city bus schedules. Our app enables real-time visibility of buses in operation, showing expected arrival times, routes, and stop-wise layouts using open transport data and GPS-based updates.

We collaborated with local depot authorities and validated the idea with real user input. The project was recognized by the Municipal Corporation for its civic impact and praised by university evaluators for its usability and potential to scale.

---

## 💡 Features

- ✅ Live bus tracking on an interactive map
- ✅ Depot-wise filtering and route visualization
- ✅ Stop and route search with autocomplete
- ✅ Mobile-responsive interface
- ✅ Clean separation of backend (logic) and frontend (display)
